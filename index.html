Our Story

About The Band

ELXR On Stage started as three friends trading songs in a garage and turned into a band that only plays what it writes. No covers, no filler — just original material built to hold up in a loud room with a live crowd in front of it.

Since forming, we've spent more nights loading gear in and out of small venues than we can count, and that's exactly how we like it. Afterglow is the first record that captures what we actually sound like live.
