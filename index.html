About The Band

ELXR On Stage started as three friends trading songs in a garage and turned into a band that only plays what it writes. No covers, no filler — just original material built to hold up in a loud room.

Meet the band: Clerasan Fernandes, Zuriel Dominic DSouza, and Judah Dominic DSouza.
